## Methodology (and why it isn't circular)

- A **good** offer is defined independently of our ranker: `rating >= 4.3` **and** `review_count >= 100` — well-rated *and* corroborated. A thin 5-star with 3 reviews is deliberately not 'good': that is precisely the trap the volume-confidence term exists to avoid.
- Offers are real, captured from live Google Shopping across 4 categories (audio, footwear, peripherals) — which also demonstrates the aggregator is genuinely multi-category, not audio-only.
- **Honest limitation:** this scores *ranking* only. It does not judge the LLM's written reasons (that is `evaluation.ragas`), and 4 recorded queries is a sanity check, not a benchmark.
