## ⚠️ Scope caveat (honest read of the data)

- The catalog is **only 9 products, all in one category (audio: headsets / earbuds / neckbands)**. This is *within-category* recommendation (pick the best audio product for a stated need: bass / battery / calls / budget), **not** a broad multi-category recommender.
- Ratings are heavily skewed to 4–5★, so `avg_rating` discriminates weakly; the ranking signal will lean more on semantic relevance + review evidence than on rating alone.
- Implication for the **Step 5 ranking-eval gate**: with 9 products, use small `k` (e.g. k=3) and weight **NDCG/MRR (ordering quality)** over Recall@k (near-trivial at this catalog size). We decide go/no-go on those numbers.
